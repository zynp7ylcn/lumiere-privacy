Privacy Policy for Lumiere – Skin Analyze

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Lumiere – Skin Analyze ("Lumiere", "we", "us", or "our") is a skincare companion application that uses information provided by users and AI-assisted processing to provide skincare insights and personalized skincare routines.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it may be processed by service providers, and the choices available to you.

Important: Lumiere provides AI-based skin insights, not a medical diagnosis. Lumiere is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create or use a Lumiere account, we may process:

  • Your email address
  • Your name, if provided
  • Your user account identifier
  • Authentication information associated with email/password login or Google Sign-In

We do not store your Google account password.

Skin Assessment Information

When you complete a skin assessment, we may process:

  • Your answers to skincare-related questions
  • Your skin assessment history
  • AI-generated skincare observations
  • Personalized skincare routine information
  • Routine completion and progress information

Skin Photos

If you choose to use Lumiere's skin analysis features, you may take or select a photo of your skin.

These photos may be:

  • Uploaded to private cloud storage
  • Associated with your Lumiere account
  • Processed to generate AI-assisted skincare insights

Lumiere does not use your skin photos for facial recognition or identity verification. Lumiere is not designed to infer your race, ethnicity, identity, or similar sensitive identity characteristics from your photos.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your Lumiere account
  • Provide skin assessments
  • Generate AI-assisted skincare insights
  • Generate personalized skincare routines
  • Display previous assessments and progress
  • Track skincare routine completion
  • Maintain application reliability and security
  • Provide account support
  • Process account and data deletion requests

3. Artificial Intelligence Processing

Lumiere uses artificial intelligence to generate skincare-related insights.

When you request an analysis, relevant information such as your skin photo and assessment information may be securely transmitted to an AI service provider for processing.

Lumiere currently uses OpenAI technology as part of its AI-assisted analysis process.

AI-generated information may be inaccurate or incomplete and should not be treated as medical advice or a medical diagnosis.

4. Service Providers

We use third-party service providers to operate Lumiere.

Supabase

Supabase provides infrastructure including:

  • User authentication
  • Database services
  • Private file storage
  • Server-side functions

Google

Google services may be used when you choose to authenticate using Google Sign-In.

OpenAI

OpenAI technology may process information submitted for AI-assisted skin analysis.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their respective services and according to their own privacy practices.

5. Camera and Photo Access

Lumiere may request permission to access your device camera or photo library when you choose to provide a skin photo.

Camera or photo access is used only for features that require a photo. Lumiere does not continuously access your camera.

6. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information.

Skin photos are stored in private cloud storage and associated with authenticated user accounts. User-specific application data is protected using authentication and database access controls.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be transmitted to service providers when necessary to operate Lumiere, including providers of authentication, cloud infrastructure, storage, and artificial intelligence processing.

We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or integrity of Lumiere and its users.

8. Data Retention

We retain account and application data while your Lumiere account remains active or while the information is reasonably necessary to provide the service.

When an account is permanently deleted, we delete or initiate deletion of personal data associated with that account, including applicable skincare assessments and uploaded skin photos.

Some information may temporarily remain in backups or system logs where technically necessary or legally required, subject to the retention processes of the relevant service providers.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You may request permanent deletion of your Lumiere account and associated data.

Account deletion may include deletion of:

  • Profile information
  • Skin assessment data
  • Assessment answers
  • AI-generated analysis results
  • Personalized skincare routines
  • Routine progress records
  • Uploaded skin photos

To request account deletion, contact us at: zyalcib1@gmail.com.

We are also preparing an in-app account deletion option before Lumiere's public Google Play release.

10. Children's Privacy

Lumiere is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If we learn that personal information has been collected in circumstances where legally required consent was not obtained, we will take appropriate steps to delete that information.

11. Your Choices

Depending on the features available to you, you may:

  • Update certain profile information
  • Choose whether to provide a skin photo
  • Control camera and photo permissions through your device settings
  • Stop using Lumiere
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Lumiere's features, service providers, or legal requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
