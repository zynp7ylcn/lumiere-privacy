Delete Your Lumiere – Skin Analyze Account

This page explains how users of Lumiere – Skin Analyze can request permanent deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your Lumiere account, send an email to:

zyalcib1@gmail.com

Please use the email address associated with your Lumiere account and include the subject:

Lumiere Account Deletion Request

We may need to verify that the request belongs to the account holder before completing deletion.

Data that will be deleted

When your account is permanently deleted, this may include:

  • Your Lumiere account and profile information
  • Skin assessment answers and history
  • AI-generated skin analysis results
  • Personalized skincare routines
  • Routine completion and progress records
  • Uploaded skin photos

Data retention

Some information may temporarily remain in secure backups or system logs where technically necessary or legally required, subject to the retention processes of the relevant service providers.

Lumiere does not treat account deactivation or freezing as account deletion.

In-app deletion

Lumiere will also provide an in-app account deletion option before its public Google Play release.
